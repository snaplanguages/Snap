Thì Hiện Tại Hoàn Thành (Present Perfect)

Chinh phục trọn vẹn lý thuyết bẫy, 65 câu ngữ pháp, hội thoại thực tế, bài đọc hiểu và luyện viết từ PDF Unit 7

1. Năm Cách Dùng Cốt Lõi Của Thì Hiện Tại Hoàn Thành

Đề thi TOEIC cực kỳ chú trọng phân biệt hành động quá khứ có liên quan đến hiện tại hay đã chấm dứt hoàn toàn:

a. Xảy ra trong quá khứ, kéo dài tới hiện tại & tiếp diễn tương lai

+ I have learned English for 10 years.

(Tôi đã học Tiếng Anh được 10 năm và hiện tại vẫn đang tiếp tục học.)

b. Xảy ra trong quá khứ nhưng KHÔNG RÕ THỜI GIAN cụ thể

+ Someone has stolen my car.

(Có ai đó đã lấy trộm ô tô của tôi - không biết chính xác lúc nào.)

c. Lặp đi lặp lại nhiều lần trong quá khứ

+ She has seen this film several times.

(Cô ấy đã xem bộ phim này vài lần rồi.)

d. Xảy ra trong quá khứ nhưng ĐỂ LẠI HẬU QUẢ / DẤU HIỆU ở hiện tại

+ The children are dirty. Where have they been?

(Bọn trẻ rất bẩn. Chúng đã ở đâu thế? ➔ Hậu quả hiện tại là "đang rất bẩn")

+ The baby is crying. Have you made fun of him?

(Em bé đang khóc. Có phải cậu vừa chọc nó không?)

e. Diễn tả kinh nghiệm, trải nghiệm

+ This is the first time I have ever eaten that kind of food.

(Đây là lần đầu tiên tôi ăn loại thức ăn đó.)

2. Hệ Thống Công Thức Cốt Lõi

Thể Công thức chuẩn Ví dụ thực tế
Khẳng định (+) S + have / has + V(p2 / V3) My mother has worked as a teacher for 10 years.
Phủ định (-) S + have / has + not + V(p2 / V3) My mother has not (hasn't) worked as a teacher for 10 years.
Nghi vấn (?) Have / Has + S + V(p2 / V3)? Has your mother worked as a teacher for 10 years?

3. Bộ Từ Khóa Dấu Hiệu Nhận Biết Tốc Độ

just (vừa mới) Đứng sau have/has, trước Vp2
already (rồi) Đứng sau have/has, trước Vp2
ever (đã từng) Đùng trong câu hỏi/so sánh nhất
never (chưa từng) Mang nghĩa phủ định
recently / lately / so far Gần đây, cho tới nay (đứng cuối)
before (trước đây) Thường đứng cuối câu
yet (chưa) Dùng trong câu phủ định & câu hỏi (đứng cuối)
for + khoảng thời gian for 3 years, for a long time...
since + mốc thời gian since 1990, since yesterday...
until now / up to now Cho đến tận bây giờ
THẦN CHÚ PHÂN BIỆT SINCE & FOR
"SINCE đi với MỐC thời gian rõ ràng (since 2010, since last year) / FOR đi với KHOẢNG thời gian đo đếm (for 5 years, for 3 hours)!"

Thử Thách Đọc Hiểu Comprehension từ PDF Unit 7 (Passages 1, 2 & 3)

Passage 1: English Learning Around the World [200710]

For many years students from all over the world have studied English. Some of these are very young people, and some others are teenagers. Many are adults. Some have learned at home, and others have studied by themselves. A few have learned English just by hearing the language in film on television, in the office or among their friends. But not many are lucky enough to do that. Most people must work hard to learn another language.

Many boys and girls learn English at school because it is one of their subjects. They study their own language, mathematics and English. In England, or America, or Australia, many boys and girls study their own language, which is English, and mathematics and another language, perhaps French, or German or Spanish. Many adults learn English because it is useful for their work. Teenagers often learn English for their higher studies, because some of their books are in English at the college or university. Other people learn English because they want to read newspapers or magazines in English.

Passage 2: Mrs. Brown & Her Grandfather [200713]

Every morning he goes for a walk in the park and comes home at half past eleven for his lunch. He usually goes with Mr. Parker, the neighbor next door.

Now it is eleven o'clock, and Mrs. Brown is cooking. A police car has just stopped at her house and Mr. Brown is getting out of the car. She is opening the door while one of the policeman is saying: "The poor old gentleman has lost his way in the park and telephoned to us for help." Mrs. Brown is a bit confused but she is happy.

She is talking to her grandfather: "You have been to that park nearly everyday for twenty years." The old man is smiling and saying: "I haven't lost my way. I have just been tired and I didn't want to walk home."

Passage 3: Anna Johnson - 13-year-old Swimmer [200716]

Anna Johnson is a 13-year-old swimmer who lives in Melbourne, Australia. She has dreamed of swimming for Australia in the Olympics for many years. She swims in both long and short races and she has already come first in many important competitions.

As well as spending many hours in the pool, Ana also makes time for studying and for friends. "I have had lots of friends who swim and we're so close. It's much easier to have friends who are swimmers because they also have to get up early to practice like me and they understand this kind of life. But I'm not so different from other people at my age. In my free time I also enjoy going to the movies and parties..."
