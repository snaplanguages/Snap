UNIT 11 • THE SIMPLE FUTURE TENSE

Thì Tương Lai Đơn (Future Simple Tense)

Làm chủ Will + V-inf, phân biệt bẫy Be going to, hội thoại giao tiếp & 3 bài đọc hiểu thiết kế 2 cột song song

1. Các Cách Dùng Của Thì Tương Lai Đơn

a. Diễn đạt một quyết định BỘC PHÁT ngay tại thời điểm nói

Nảy ra tức thời ngay lúc nói, hoàn toàn KHÔNG CÓ kế hoạch hay chuẩn bị từ trước.

+ We will see what we can do to help you.
(Chúng tôi sẽ xem để có thể giúp gì cho anh.)

b. Diễn đạt một dự đoán KHÔNG CÓ CĂN CỨ rõ ràng

Dự đoán cá nhân chỉ dựa trên suy nghĩ, cảm tính hoặc phỏng đoán chủ quan.

+ I think she will come to the meeting on time.
(Tôi nghĩ là cô ấy sẽ đến cuộc họp đúng giờ.)

c. Diễn đạt một lời HỨA HẸN hoặc ĐỀ NGHỊ/NHỜ VẢ

Dùng để hứa thực hiện điều gì đó hoặc ngỏ lời giúp đỡ/yêu cầu lịch sự.

+ Don't worry. I will help you.
(Đừng lo. Mình sẽ giúp bạn.)

2. Công Thức Chuẩn Của Thì Tương Lai Đơn

(+) Khẳng định: S + WILL + V (nguyên dạng)
(-) Phủ định: S + WILL NOT (WON'T) + V (nguyên dạng)
(?) Nghi vấn: WILL + S + V (nguyên dạng)?
Thể Khẳng Định (+) Thể Phủ Định (-) Thể Nghi Vấn (?)
I will help her take care of her children tomorrow. I won't tell her the truth. Will you come here tomorrow?

Bẫy bít mắt: Phân biệt WILL vs BE GOING TO

  • 1. WILL (Tương lai đơn): Quyết định bộc phát ngay tại thời điểm nói, không có kế hoạch trước; hoặc dự đoán KHÔNG CÓ BẰNG CHỨNG (I think, I hope, I believe...).
  • 2. BE GOING TO (Tương lai gần): Đã CÓ KẾ HOẠCH/DỰ ĐỊNH từ trước; hoặc dự đoán CÓ BẰNG CHỨNG RÕ RÀNG ở hiện tại (VD: Nhìn mây đen kìa -> It is going to rain).

3. Dấu Hiệu Nhận Biết & Thần Chú Mẹo Nhớ

✨ 1. Trạng từ chỉ thời gian trong tương lai:

tomorrow (ngày mai) next day / week / month / year (hôm/tuần/tháng/năm tới) in + thời gian (in 2 minutes - trong 2 phút nữa) in the future (trong tương lai)

✨ 2. Các động từ & trạng từ chỉ quan điểm, phỏng đoán:

I think / believe / suppose / guess (tôi nghĩ/tin/cho là) I promise / hope / expect (tôi hứa/hy vọng/mong) perhaps / probably / maybe (có lẽ) I'm sure / I'm afraid (tôi chắc chắn/tôi e rằng)
MẸO THẦN CHÚ "WILL + V-BARE"
1. Thần chú động từ trần trụi: Đã xài chữ WILL thì động từ phía sau lột sạch! Không chia s/es, không -ed, không -ing, không "to". Luôn là V-nguyên thể!
2. Thần chú tín hiệu 100%: Cứ thấy I think / I hope / I promise / Perhaps + mốc tương lai -> Tự tin chọn ngay WILL!

1. Conversation 1: Future Life (Cuộc Sống Trong Tương Lai)

Fortune teller: Let me tell you what your future will be.
Mrs Amber: Oh...Why not? Let's see....
Fortune teller: You are young and pretty...You are also very nice. Very soon, you'll meet a man...a very handsome man. He'll fall in love with you....
Mrs Amber: Really? How interesting!
Fortune teller: He is a rich businessman. He'll marry you when you've known each other for 5 months.
Mrs Amber: Fascinating! Tell me more!
Fortune teller: You'll have children and be very happy together.
Mrs Amber: Tremendous! What else?
Fortune teller: You'll be a powerful woman because you'll be the CEO of a big company.
Mrs Amber: Well, I am so excited about that. I can't wait.... When will I meet him?
Fortune teller: Ah...I will tell you when you give me money.
Mrs Amber: So, then....thank you! Goodbye...
Fortune teller: What about my money?
Mrs Amber: You will get it when you tell me where I can find my wedding ring which I have just lost! You see I've been married for five years and already have two children.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Future Life)

• fortune teller (n): thầy bói
• fortune (n): vận may, tài lộc
• fall in love = love (v): đem lòng yêu
• fascinating (adj): tuyệt vời, hấp dẫn
• fascinated (adj): bị mê hoặc
• powerful (adj): đầy quyền lực
• tremendous (adj): khủng khiếp, dữ dội
• excited (adj): hào hứng
• be married (vp): kết hôn
• CEO (n): giám đốc điều hành

2. Conversation 2: Travelling (Việc Di Chuyển)

A: Good morning.
B: Good morning. Are you going somewhere?
A: Yes. I am going to the city to do some shopping and have lunch.
B: That sounds fun. How will you get there?
A: I don't want to take the train. I will ride a bicycle.
B: Don't you like using the train?
A: The train station is far away and riding my bicycle does not cost anything.
B: Ok. I see. Why don't you travel by bus, there is a bus stop just behind the university.
A: It might take longer. I think I will need to take two buses to the city centre.
B: You are right. There is not a direct bus into the city. Let's use a taxi.
A: That will be quick, but expensive. Can you rollerblade?
B: I hope you don't mean you will rollerblade to the city.
A: I will if possible because in my country rollerblading is popular.
B: I think I will try in the future.
A: You should. It's quite interesting but a bit tiring.
B: I need to go to the train station to buy some tickets. I will give you both a lift in my car.
A: That would be wonderful. Thanks so much. It's very kind of you. Let's go to the city centre!

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Travelling)

• Why don't you...? : Tại sao bạn không...? (Gợi ý)
• Let's... : Hãy cùng làm gì
• direct (adj): đi thẳng, trực tiếp
• rollerblade (v): trượt ván / pa-tin
• train station (n): ga tàu hỏa
• popular (adj): phổ biến
• give someone a lift (vp): cho ai đó đi nhờ
• It's very kind of someone: Ai đó thật tốt bụng
• means of transportation (np): phương tiện giao thông
• public transport (n): phương tiện công cộng

📖 Passage 1: Space Holiday Centre (ID 201110)

The World Tourism Organisation says that the space will soon (1)______ a popular place to go on holiday – possibly by 2030. One Japanese company is getting ready. They are planning a space holiday centre. It will look (2)______ a bicycle wheel and have a hotel for 100 people and a theme park. It will travel around the Earth at a height of 300 km. The company (3)______ the centre will be ready in 2027.

The company chairman says he thinks that flying to the centre will probably be quicker than flying from Hong Kong to Singapore. When they are on board the centre, (4)______ will take short trips to the moon and go for walks in space. But what about the (5)______? Well, the company believes that people will be happy to pay about $100,000 for a trip but they think that as more people want to go, the trip will become much cheaper.

An American hotel group is also (6)______ of building a hotel, called the Lunar Hotel. This will be on the moon. Most of the hotel will be under the ground so it won't become too hot or too cold. The (7)______ will look just like they do on Earth with curtains, carpets, plants and they will have wall-to-wall television. The guests will eat normal food for lunch. The cooks will just (8)______ a button to mix dried food with water to become an instant meal!

📖 Passage 2: Life in 2035 (ID 201113)

This is what young people say about life in 2035.

Sam, 26: "I think a lot of things will change in 2035. India will be the first largest economy in the world, but the country will have many problems with its demographics. The world will also have a lot of problems with pollution and the climate changing. We will need to find other resources, because there will be more than 8 billion people on Earth in 2035. We will also need to build spaceships to travel to the Moon and to Mars, because we will have to take resources from these places."

Kela, 21: "Our world won't be the same in 2035. We will improve our technology and everybody will be able to speak at least five languages. People will live longer and we won't have health problems, because we will improve our knowledge on medicine. We will also live in peace and the different countries won't have nuclear weapons anymore. We will also have a single world government as we already have the International Monetary Fund. I think 2035 will be a great year for mankind."

Alex, 30: "I hope the world countries will be able to overcome the different issues in 2035. We will live in peace and won't have any wars anymore. We will be able to explore the space and to find other habitable planets. We will perhaps find other intelligent Beings on other planets in 2035."

📖 Passage 3: Smart Homes in the Future (ID 201110)

Technology will allow homes in the future to be "smart". Appliances will communicate with each other - and with you. Your stove, for instance, will tell you when your food is cooked and ready to eat. Refrigerators will suggest recipes based on food items you already have.

Technology is possible because of tiny information-storing devices called RFID chips. People already use them to keep track of pets and farm animals. Future RFID chips will store information about all the items in your cabinets. For example, they will record the date you bought each item. Other devices will read this information using radio waves. When you need more food, your cabinets will tell you to buy it.

In a smart house, you won't have to repaint them. The walls will actually be digital screens, like computer or TV screens. The technology is called OLED, and it's here already. OLEDs are tiny devices that use electricity to light things. You can find the same technology in today's thin TV screens. OLED walls will become clear, like windows, or display colors and patterns, like walls.

A computer network will link these walls with everything else in your house. Called "ambient intelligence," this computer brain will control your entire house. It will also adapt to your preferences. Your house will learn about your likes and dislikes. It will then use that knowledge to control the environment. For example, it will set the heat in the house to your favourite temperature. It will turn on the shower at the right temperature. It will also darken the windows at night and lighten them when it's time to wake up.

(Adapted from Pathway)
