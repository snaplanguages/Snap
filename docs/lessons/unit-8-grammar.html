Thì Quá Khứ Đơn (Past Simple Tense)

🕰️ Tiếng Anh Cho Người Bắt Đầu cùng SnapEnglish | The Past Simple Tense 🕰️

1. Các Cách Dùng Của Thì Quá Khứ Đơn

a. Diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra và ĐÃ CHẤM DỨT hoàn toàn trong quá khứ

Hành động không còn liên quan tới hiện tại (thường có mốc thời gian rõ ràng như in 2000, yesterday, last week, ago...)

+ We bought the house in 2000. (Chúng tôi mua ngôi nhà đó năm 2000.)

b. Diễn đạt các hành động xảy ra LIÊN TIẾP trong quá khứ

Chuỗi hành động kể lại theo trình tự thời gian.

+ She turned on her computer, read the message on Facebook and answered it.
(Cô ấy bật máy tính, đọc tin nhắn trên Facebook rồi trả lời nó.)

c. Diễn tả hồi ức, kỷ niệm, thói quen trong quá khứ

Thói quen trong quá khứ nay đã chấm dứt (thường dùng kèm used to + V).

+ When I was small, I used to go fishing in the afternoon.
(Hồi còn nhỏ, tôi thường đi câu cá vào buổi chiều.)

2. Công Thức & Quy Tắc Biến Đổi

S + V(ed) / V(cột 2) / To-be (was/were)
✨ Động Từ Thường (Normal Verbs) ✨ Động Từ To-Be
(+) Khẳng định: S + V(ed) / V2
(-) Phủ định: S + did not (didn't) + V-inf
(?) Nghi vấn: Did + S + V-inf?

Ví dụ:
• I played football yesterday.
• He didn't come to school last week.
• Did she come to school? 		• was: đi với I, He, She, It, Danh từ số ít.
• were: đi với We, You, They, Danh từ số nhiều.

(-) Phủ định: S + wasn't / weren't
(?) Nghi vấn: Was / Were + S ...?

Ví dụ:
• I was at home last night.
• They weren't happy with the result.

Quy tắc thêm đuôi "-ed" chuẩn chính tả

  • 1. Tận cùng là "-e": Chỉ việc thêm "-d" (type ➔ typed | love ➔ loved | like ➔ liked).
  • 2. Từ 1 âm tiết (1 nguyên âm + 1 phụ âm): Gấp đôi phụ âm cuối (stop ➔ stopped | shop ➔ shopped | tap ➔ tapped).
  • 3. Từ 2 âm tiết:
    - Trọng âm âm 1 ➔ Chỉ thêm "-ed" (listen ➔ listened | enter ➔ entered | cancel ➔ canceled).
    - Trọng âm âm 2 ➔ Gấp đôi phụ âm (commit ➔ committed | prefer ➔ preferred).
  • 4. Tận cùng là "-y":
    - Trước "y" là nguyên âm (U,E,O,A,I - Uể Oải) ➔ Thêm "-ed" (play ➔ played | stay ➔ stayed).
    - Trước "y" là phụ âm ➔ Đổi "y" thành "i" rồi thêm "-ed" (study ➔ studied | cry ➔ cried).

3. Dấu Hiệu Nhận Biết & Thần Chú Phòng Thi

Yesterday (hôm qua) Last night / week / month / year ... ago (two hours ago, 2 weeks ago) In + mốc năm quá khứ (in 1998, in 2000, in 2015) When I was young / When (trong câu kể hồi ức)
THẦN CHÚ "KẺ CẮP MẶT TRĂNG" & PHÁT ÂM -ED
1. Định lý "Kẻ cướp": Đã mượn trợ động từ DID / DIDN'T thì Động từ chính bị "cướp" đuôi -ed/V2 và bắt buộc trở về dạng nguyên mẫu (V-inf)!
2. Thần chú phát âm đuôi -ED:
• Phát âm là /id/: Tận cùng là t, d (Thần chú: "Tiền đô").
• Phát âm là /t/: Tận cùng là p, k, f, s, sh, ch, x, c, ce (Thần chú: "Chó sủa xôn xao khắp phố phường").
• Phát âm là /d/: Các trường hợp còn lại.

1. Conversation 1: Holiday (Kỳ Nghỉ)

A: Hi, Linh. Nice to meet you again after a long holiday.
B: Hello, Nam. Nice to meet you, too.
A: You look strong and sunburnt. Where did you go on last holiday?
B: Well. I went to Thailand. It's a wonderful place with busy cities, exciting nightlife, beautiful beaches and great food.
A: Wow. It sounds great. What did you do there?
B: My family and I spent 2 days in Bangkok, the capital city. We went shopping in the mega malls, and we bought a lot of clothes, shoes and bags. Also, we walked along big streets where we could enjoy street food and ice-cream.
A: So cool! Did you go to Pattaya?
B: Yes. We went there on the second day of the trip. I swam and surfed on the beach when I was there. It is a beautiful beach.
A: What else did you do there?
B: The next morning we had a cruise around the sea on a boat. It was a beautiful experience to see the coral.
A: Is it beautiful under the sea?
B: Absolutely! I really want to do it again. What did you do before you left Thailand?
B: On the last day, I visited the Palace and the Museum. There are a lot of wonderful and unique things there.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Holiday)

• strong (adj): mạnh khỏe
• sunburnt (adj): rám nắng
• wonderful (adj): tuyệt vời
• exciting (adj): náo nhiệt, thú vị
• nightlife (n): cuộc sống về đêm
• mega (adj): lớn, khổng lồ
• surf (v): lướt sóng
• coral (n): san hô
• palace (n): lâu đài, cung điện
• cruise (n): chuyến đi biển bằng tàu

2. Conversation 2: Past Life (Tuổi Thơ)

A: Hey. What are you doing?
B: I am looking at some childhood photos of us. I found them yesterday. There are some nice ones.
A: Childhood photos? Let me see. Well, these remind about our memories. Do you remember our village?
B: Of course. We were in a small village by a river and we used to spend time playing around.
A: Yes. How nice those days were! In the morning, we woke up early, had breakfast and went to the field to help parents grow vegetables. Then, in the afternoon, we played hide-and-seek, flied kites or swam in the river.
B: Yes. In the evening the children in our village gathered in our house and played cards. Then, at night time grandpa used to tell us fairy tales. They were fantastic tales.
A: And grandma often baked our favourite sweet potatoes.
B: I have still remembered the taste of those potatoes. They were so yummy. We were so active in our childhood. There were no TV, no computers and no mobile phones. Children were close to one another and could experience the most wonderful time in one's life.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Childhood)

• childhood (n): thời thơ ấu
• remind (v): gợi nhớ, nhắc nhở
• used to + V: đã từng / thường làm gì
• wake up - woke up: thức dậy
• gather (v): tập hợp, tụ họp
• fairy tale (np): truyện cổ tích
• bake (v): nướng (bằng lò)
• sweet potato (n): củ khoai lang

📖 Passage 3: Mary's Running Career (ID 200816)

When Mary injured her back she had to take a break from her running career. She decided to introduce more women to sport, to show them how much fun it can be and to give them the confidence to get out and run.

She decided to start a running club for women in her area because she was annoyed by the attitude of many race organizers. They complained about the lack of women in the sport but also used this as an excuse for not providing separate changing facilities.

She put up posters and 40 women, young and old, fit and unfit, joined. All of them were attracted by the idea of losing weight but she didn't think they had really thought about running before. When or if they did, they had a picture of painful training. They didn't think of chatting and smiling while running in beautiful places, like by a river.

At first they ran for only one minute - now they can run for thirty minutes. They also learned from other runners about diet and keeping fit in general.

She wanted to do something for women's running and she was pleased that women love running.
