Thì Quá Khứ Tiếp Diễn

Bản chất, công thức, quy tắc phối thì When/While và bẫy rèn luyện phản xạ siêu tốc

1. Các Bản Chất Cách Dùng Thì Quá Khứ Tiếp Diễn

a. Diễn đạt hành động ĐANG XẢY RA tại MỘT THỜI ĐIỂM CỤ THỂ trong quá khứ

Nếu quá khứ đơn chỉ cần "hôm qua", thì quá khứ tiếp diễn đòi hỏi sự chính xác: mấy giờ ngày hôm qua.

+ We were having dinner at 7 pm yesterday.
(Vào lúc 7 giờ tối qua, chúng tôi đang dùng bữa.)

b. Diễn đạt HAI hành động đang xảy ra ĐỒNG THỜI (Song song) trong quá khứ

Cả hai hành động cùng kéo dài song song, thường liên kết bằng chữ WHILE.

+ While I was doing my homework, my sister was listening to music.
(Trong khi tôi đang làm bài tập thì chị gái tôi đang nghe nhạc.)

c. Diễn đạt hành động ĐANG XẢY RA (Dài) thì có HÀNH ĐỘNG KHÁC XEN VÀO (Ngắn/Cắt ngang)

Hành động dài đang diễn ra chia Quá Khứ Tiếp Diễn, hành động ngắn xen vào chia Quá Khứ Đơn.

+ When she was having a bath, the telephone rang.
(Trong khi cô ấy đang tắm thì chuông điện thoại reo lên.)

2. Công Thức & Quy Tắc Was vs Were

(+) S + was / were + V-ing
(-) S + was / were + not + V-ing (wasn't / weren't)
(?) Was / Were + S + V-ing?
✨ WAS (3 chữ cái - Ngắn) ✨ WERE (4 chữ cái - Dài)
Đi với 主 ngữ SỐ ÍT:
I, He, She, It, Danh từ số ít, Danh từ không đếm được.

Ví dụ: I was reading books at 9 pm yesterday. 		Đi với 主 ngữ SỐ NHIỀU:
We, You, They, Danh từ số nhiều.

Ví dụ: They were playing cards at 8:15 pm.

Quy tắc phân vai diễn xuất: WHEN & WHILE

  • 1. WHEN (Khi / Lúc): Thường đi kề trước Hành động cắt ngang (Ngắn) → Mệnh đề sau WHEN chia Quá khứ đơn (V2/ed).
  • 2. WHILE (Trong khi): Thường đi kề trước Hành động đang diễn ra (Dài) → Mệnh đề sau WHILE chia Quá khứ tiếp diễn (was/were + V-ing).

3. Dấu Hiệu Nhận Biết & Thần Chú Phòng Thi

At + giờ + thời gian quá khứ (at 7 pm yesterday, at 12 o'clock last night) At this time + thời gian quá khứ (at this time yesterday, at this time 2 weeks ago) When / While trong câu kể hai hành động kết hợp
MẸO THẦN CHÚ "CẮT NGANG vs SONG SONG"
1. Thần chú chữ ngắn chữ dài: WAS (3 chữ) đi số ít, WERE (4 chữ) đi số nhiều!
2. Thần chú hành động: Hành động DÀI đang làm thì WAS/WERE + V-ING / Hành động NGẮN cắt ngang thì V2/ED cắt qua!
3. Mẹo phân vai: Sau WHILE ưu tiên V-ING, sau WHEN ưu tiên V2/ED!

1. Conversation 1: Weather (Thời Tiết)

A: Hey, did you go out for dinner yesterday?
B: Yes. I went to a famous restaurant with my friend, but unfortunately, it rained last night.
A: Didn't you watch the weather forecast?
B: I did. We were going to the restaurant by motorbike when it rained. We all got wet.
A: Poor you. I got a similar situation last year when I had a trip to Nha Trang with my family.
B: What happened then?
A: We prepared carefully for the trip, followed the weather forecast. However, it wasn't always right. At first, it predicted that it would be sunny and hot there. We believed that we would go swimming and enjoy the time there because the weather was good.
B: What was the weather like when you were there?
A: On the first day we were there, it was sunny in the morning and we could enjoy the atmosphere on the beach. At 6 a.m. the sun was shining and we were swimming in the sea. Then, it became dark because of the black clouds at noon. The wind was blowing and the weather was changing soon.
B: Did it rain?
A: Yes, of course. It started to rain when we were sitting in a café. The next two days it didn't stop raining, and the weather forecast said there was a sudden storm. We couldn't go to Vinpearl Land or any other places. We stayed in the room hotel and watched the rain outside.
B: How unlucky you were!
A: Yes. Otherwise, we had time together and we talked more.
B: You're right. Every cloud has a silver lining.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Weather)

• weather forecast (n): dự báo thời tiết
• shine (v): chiếu sáng (sun was shining)
• blow (v): thổi (wind was blowing)
• thunderstorm (n): dông kèm sấm sét
• hurricane / storm (n): bão lớn
• humid (adj): ẩm ướt, nồm ẩm
• Every cloud has a silver lining: Trong cái rủi có cái may

2. Conversation 2: Restaurants (Nhà Hàng)

A: Hey, I saw you at Seafood Restaurant last weekend.
B: Really? I didn't see you. We were celebrating my mother's birthday.
A: I was there with my family, too. We like to eat out at weekends.
B: Can you cook?
A: Yes. I really enjoy cooking but I also look forward to letting a chef prepare a meal.
B: I think you are an excellent cook but if you go out, you do not have to do the washing up.
A: You are right. However, it's difficult to decide where to go as there are so many good restaurants locally.
B: We had a very good meal last week at the Chinese restaurant. The chef was cooking in front of us while we were enjoying the food and watching him.
A: That sounds great. Do you like Indian food?
B: Well, I prefer Thai cooking. Thai food is delicious. I find Indian food is too spicy and the chillies burn my throat.
B: Why don't we have dinner party together today? We can go to a newly opened Thai restaurant in Spring Avenue.
A: That's a good idea. I'm free tonight. Shall I phone the Thai restaurant and see if we can make a reservation.
B: Yes please. What time suits you? I don't get home from work until 7 p.m., so I think 8 o'clock would be fine.

Từ vựng & Cấu trúc diễn đạt (Restaurants)

• eat out (vp): ăn ở nhà hàng / bên ngoài
• celebrate (v): tổ chức kỷ niệm
• look forward to V-ing: mong đợi làm gì
• do the washing up (vp): rửa bát đĩa
• chef (n): đầu bếp chuyên nghiệp
• make a reservation: đặt bàn / chỗ trước
• spicy (adj): cay xè (chilli)
• convenient (adj): thuận tiện

📖 Passage 1: Mr. & Mrs. Parker's Evening (ID 200911)

Last night Mr. and Mrs. Parker stayed at home. It was cold and rainy and they didn't want to go out in the bad weather. Mrs. Parker made some snacks. At 8:00, Mr. and Mrs. Parker went into the living room, sat on the sofa and began to watch the movie. At 8:15, they realized that they already watched the movie and they didn't want to watch it again.

"What should we do now?" asked Mr. Parker. "Let's play cards!" answered Mrs. Parker. They started to play cards when it was raining heavily. They were playing for 1 hour when Mrs. Parker looked out the window. The rain changed to snow and the trees were covered with beautiful white snowflakes. "It looks beautiful outside but this weather is too dangerous to go out. It's good to stay at home tonight," Mr. Parker said. "You're right, Mrs. Parker said, "but I'm bored. Let's make some cookies."

Mr. Parker was surprised "Cookies? But it's rather late now. Why don't we dance and sing?"

"Oh, come on!" Mrs. Parker insisted. They went into the kitchen, put the ingredients on the table. They were mixing the flour when suddenly the lights went out. Mrs. Parker laughed. "We are just not lucky tonight," she said. Mr. Parker said: "Oh no, we are lucky! We are here together!" He turned the light of his mobile phone on to help her clear the table. They had a wonderful night.

(Source: Tiếng Anh cho người bắt đầu - Moon.vn)

📖 Passage 2: Howard Bonnier's Restaurant (ID 200915)

Paragraph A: Bray is a beautiful village about fifty kilometers west of London. A young Englishman named Howard Bonnier opened a restaurant called The Palace there about three and a half months ago. Not many people in Britain know Mr. Bonnier's name yet, but he's already famous in France. This is because he was written in French magazines about almost all the best restaurants in that country. He's only 29 years old.

Paragraph B: When Howard was a teenager, he often went to restaurants with his mother and father. He liked doing this so much that he decided not to buy lots of clothes and CDs; instead, he used his money to visit France and eat in good restaurants. He also bought a lot of French and English cookbooks - he says he has more than two hundred and fifty.

Paragraph C: So why did he decide to open a restaurant? Simply because he loves cooking. Has it been an easy thing to do? He says it's expensive to start your own restaurant and it's much more difficult to cook for fifty people than to cook for your family, but he's sure he's done the right thing.

Paragraph D: He has tried a lot to make delicious French and British food. His passion of cooking and what he achieved in the cooking has inspired the young who always keep going on the path they have chosen.

(Adapted from KET)

📖 Passage 3: History of Popcorn (ID 200919)

Indians in North and South America ate popcorn thousands of years ago. Scientists found some ears of popcorn in New Mexico, a state in the United States. They were 5,600 years old. Farmers probably learned to raise popcorn first before they planted other kinds of corn. Farmers now raise popcorn in the United States, Australia, Argentina, South Africa and southern Europe.

Corn was an important food for the Indians. It was also important for their religion. When Columbus and other Europeans visited the New World, they saw this. When the Indians and Europeans had their first Thanksgiving, they ate popcorn. Today Thanksgiving is an important holiday in the United States but people don't usually eat popcorn for this holiday now. Many Europeans and Indians fought wars with each other. When a war finished, the Indians brought popcorn as a sign of peace.

In the 1920s, people started selling popcorn at movies. Now most movie theaters in the United States sell popcorn. Popcorn and movies go together very well. During the Second World War, American soldiers in the army taught Europeans to eat popcorn.

Is popcorn good for you? Yes, it is. However, some people put a lot of salt and butter or vegetable oil on it. It tastes good that way, but it is not very good for you.

(Adapted from TiengAnh 7)
