Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành

Giải mã quy luật "Hành động xảy ra TRƯỚC - SAU" & bẻ gẫy bẫy liên từ Before, After, By the time

1. Cách Dùng Cốt Lõi (Trục Thời Gian)

Quy Luật Bất Di Bất Dịch: TRƯỚC vs SAU

Thì Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành diễn tả hành động đã xảy ra và hoàn thành TRƯỚC một hành động khác (hoặc trước một mốc thời điểm) trong quá khứ.

+ When my grandfather retired last year, he had worked for the company for more than 40 years.
(Khi ông tôi nghỉ hưu vào năm ngoái, ông đã làm việc cho công ty ấy được hơn 40 năm.)
💡 Giải thích trục thời gian:
1. "Worked" (Làm việc): Xảy ra TRƯỚC ➔ Dùng Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành (had worked).
2. "Retired" (Nghỉ hưu): Xảy ra SAU ➔ Dùng Quá Khứ Đơn (retired).

2. Công Thức Chuẩn (Formulas)

(+) Khẳng định: S + had + V3/ed (p2)
(-) Phủ định: S + had not (hadn't) + V3/ed (p2)
(?) Nghi vấn: Had + S + V3/ed (p2)?

(+): He had gone out when I came home.

(Anh ấy đã đi ra ngoài trước khi tôi về nhà.)

(-): She hadn't come home when I came home.

(Cô ấy vẫn chưa về nhà khi tôi về nhà.)

(?): Had the film ended when you arrived at the cinema?

(Bộ phim đã kết thúc trước khi bạn tới rạp chiếu phim phải không?)

3. Dấu Hiệu Nhận Biết & Cấu Trúc Bẫy Cần Thuộc

✨ BEFORE / BY THE TIME (Trước khi / Vào lúc) ✨ AFTER (Sau khi)
Mệnh đề đi liền ngay sau Before / By the time chia Quá Khứ Đơn.
Mệnh đề còn lại chia Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành.

Before + S + V2/ed, S + had + V3/ed
Ví dụ: Before I went home, I had finished all my work.

By the time + S + V2/ed, S + had + V3/ed
Ví dụ: By the time you arrived at the station, the train had left. 		Ngược lại hoàn toàn!
Mệnh đề đi liền ngay sau After chia Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành.
Mệnh đề còn lại chia Quá Khứ Đơn.

After + S + had + V3/ed, S + V2/ed
Ví dụ: What did you do after you had graduated from university?

Ám hiệu đặc biệt với giới từ "BY"

Hễ thấy xuất hiện "BY" + một mốc thời gian quá khứ (như: By yesterday, By 2010, By the end of last month, By last year...) ➔ 99% câu đó phải chia Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành!

By the end of last month, we had worked here for 20 years.

(Tính đến cuối tháng trước, chúng tôi đã làm việc ở đây được 20 năm.)

MẸO THẦN CHÚ "BÁM ĐUÔI" PHÒNG THI
1. Thần chú bám đuôi After: Chữ AFTER (sau) mang nghĩa hoàn tất ➔ Ngay sau "After" hãy dán chặt Quá Khứ Hoàn Thành (After + had + V3)!
2. Thần chú bám đuôi Before: Chữ BEFORE / BY THE TIME (trước khi) mang nghĩa tiếp nối ➔ Ngay sau nó hãy dán chặt Quá Khứ Đơn (Before + V2/ed)!

1. Conversation 1: Celebrity (Người Nổi Tiếng)

A: Linh, have you ever seen a famous person?
B: Yes. I had met some famous music stars by the time I worked in a restaurant last year.
A: What did you do? Did you take a photo with them?
B: No. They were not of my interest and I wanted to leave them alone to enjoy meals with their families.
A: That's good. You know I met Johnny, a famous actor, many years ago. And like you I did not disturb him.
B: You did? I like his films and I'm a big fan of him.
A: Yes. His acting was so natural. He was handsome and manly. However, it's a pity he passed away because of an accident.
B: As far as I know he had appeared in more than 30 films before he died.
A: Yes. He was a talented actor.
B: I remember that he had received Oscar awards twice by 2010.
A: I know. He's the best actor I've ever seen.

Từ Vựng & Cấu Trúc Trọng Tâm (Celebrity)

• celebrity (n): người nổi tiếng
• music star (np): ngôi sao ca nhạc
• be of one's interest: là mối quan tâm của ai
• disturb (v): quấy rầy, làm phiền
• a big fan of (np): fan hâm mộ cuồng nhiệt
• acting (n): diễn xuất
• pass away (vp) = die: qua đời, qua đời
• talented (adj): tài năng
• Oscar award (np): giải thưởng Oscar
• admire (v): ngưỡng mộ, khâm phục

2. Conversation 2: City Life (Cuộc Sống Thành Thị)

A: Mai, are you writing an essay about city life?
B: Yes. It's my homework for English writing. I am writing about city life in the past and now.
A: That sounds good. Did you find a lot of interesting information?
B: Of course. I had searched it on the internet before I started to write.
A: What are the key points in your essay?
B: I am focusing on the differences of people's thoughts and technology. Before the 1960s people had got married early. The average age of wedded couples was 20 for women and 23 for men.
A: How young they were! What about technology?
B: People had gone to the bank to get cash before the ATMs were commonplace. Cars and motorbikes were very different 50 years ago. Safety regulations were too. You hadn't been required by law to wear a seat belt or helmets by 2004. Many cars also didn't have airbags yet.
A: Yes. I still remember the days when I rode a motorbike without a helmet.
B: Smartphones are common now, but rotary phones had been the best way of communication by the time I was born. Sometimes people got crazy to dial number again and again. One more thing, the drinking age had been 18 before it changed to 21 in 1984.

Từ Vựng & Cấu Trúc Trọng Tâm (City Life)

• essay (n): bài tiểu luận
• get married (vp): kết hôn
• commonplace (adj): phổ biến, đại đà
• seat belt (n): dây an toàn
• helmet (n): mũ bảo hiểm
• rotary phone (np): điện thoại quay số
• dial (v): quay số điện thoại
• cash (n): tiền mặt

📖 Passage 2: Charles Dickens (ID 201014)

Charles Dickens was an English writer. Dickens was born on February 7, 1812 in Portsmouth, on the southern coast of England. His father, John Dickens, was a clerk who dreamed of becoming rich and his mother, Elizabeth Barrow, aspired to be a teacher and school director. But the family's financial situation had grown worse because John Dickens used to spend money dangerously beyond the family's means. Consequently, John was sent to prison for debt in 1824, when Charles was just 12 years old.

After his father's imprisonment, Charles Dickens left school to work in a factory. Despite his lack of formal education, he edited a weekly journal for 20 years, wrote 15 novels, five novellas, hundreds of short stories and non-fiction articles.

Dickens's literary success began with the 1836 serial publication of The Pickwick Papers. Within a few years he had become an international literary celebrity. Dickens was regarded as the literary colossus of his age. His 1843 novella, A Christmas Carol, remains popular and continues to inspire adaptations in every artistic genre. Oliver Twist and Great Expectations are also frequently adapted. His 1859 novel, A Tale of Two Cities, set in London and Paris, is his best-known work of historical fiction.

Charles Dickens died on June 9, 1870 of a stroke. He was buried in Poet's Corner at Westminster Abbey, London. Thousands of mourners came to pay their respects at the grave and throw in flowers.

(Adapted from MyEnglishpage)

📖 Passage 3: City of Detroit (ID 201017)

The city of Detroit, in the USA, was once compared to Paris. It had a broad river, smart streets and historically important architecture. Then, in the 20th century, it became 'Motor City'. For a time, most of the world's cars were made here. There was regular work and a good salary in the motor industry. A worker at one of the car factories could own a home, plus a boat, maybe even a holiday cottage. Some say America's middle class was born in Detroit - new highways certainly made it easy for workers to move from the city center to the suburbs in the 1950s. But in the early years of the 21st century, Detroit became America's poorest big city.

In less than five decades the once lively Motor City lost more than half its population. It became known as a city that was failing, full of ruined buildings, extensive poverty and crime. Newspapers and magazines told stories of derelict homes and empty streets. Photographers went to Detroit to record the strange beauty of buildings and city blocks where nature was taking over again. What went wrong in Detroit?

The city is now 69th among US cities for the number of people per square mile. The population fell for several reasons. Partly, it was because people had moved to the suburbs in the 1950s. Then there were the shocking riots in 1967, which scared more people away from the city. Then there was the dramatic fall in car manufacture as companies like General Motors and Chrysler faced huge difficulties. And finally, in 2008, came the global financial crisis. Many of Detroit's people are poor - half of the city's families live on less than 25,000 dollars a year.

(Adapted from National Geographic Learning)
