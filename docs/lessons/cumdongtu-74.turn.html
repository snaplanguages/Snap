Unit 74: TURN

Spin your English skills to perfection!
🚫 / 🙈
Turn away
Không cho vào / Quay mặt đi = refuse entry / look in another direction
"Thousands of applicants are turned away each year."
"When they show an operation on TV, I have to turn away."
💡 Tip: Away = Hướng ra xa (Từ chối cho vào hoặc ngoảnh mặt đi).
↩️
Turn back
Quay trở lại = return the way you have come
"We're lost - we'll have to turn back."
💡 Tip: Back = Quay ngược lại tuyến đường cũ.
🔉 / 🙅‍♂️
Turn down
Giảm âm lượng, nhiệt độ / Từ chối = reduce noise/heat; reject an offer/proposal
"The room was too hot, so she turned the heating down."
"They offered her the job, but she turned it down."
💡 Tip: Down = Hạ nhiệt độ/âm lượng hoặc bác bỏ đề nghị.
🛌 / 📄 / 👮‍♂️
Turn in
Đi ngủ / Gửi, trả lại / Giao nộp = go to bed / give back st / hand in to authority
"I usually turn in at about midnight."
"She turned in her paper."
"My wallet was turned in to the police two days later."
💡 Tip: In = Chui vào chăn ngủ hoặc nộp vào trong.
🔌 / ↪️
Turn off
Tắt máy, thiết bị / Rẽ sang đường khác = stop flow of power/water; leave a road
"I turned the TV off and went to bed."
"Turn off the motorway at the next exit."
💡 Tip: Off = Ngắt kết nối thiết bị / Quẹo khỏi tuyến đường chính.
💡 / 😍 / 🐕⚡
Turn on
Bật máy / Tạo hứng thú / Tấn công = start device; excite sb; attack unexpectedly
"I turned the radio on to get the forecast."
"He really turns me on."
"The neighbour's dog turned on me."
💡 Tip: On = Bật công tắc / Kích hoạt / Lao vào tấn công.
✨ / 👥
Turn out
Hóa ra là / Tham gia, đến dự = result in a way; be present at an event
"As events turned out, we were right to leave early."
"Thousands turned out for the demonstration."
💡 Tip: Out = Bộc lộ kết quả / Kéo ra ngoài tham dự.
⚙️ / 🚗🔄
Turn over
Khởi động (động cơ) / Lật úp = engine runs/starts; flip position
"The engine turned over twice and then stopped."
"The car skidded and turned over."
💡 Tip: Over = Lật úp mặt lại / Trục cơ xoay tròn.
🤝 / 🍺
Turn to
Tr trông cậy / Chuyển sang thói quen = seek help/advice; start a new habit/substance
"She has nobody she can turn to."
"He turned to drink after he lost his job."
💡 Tip: To = Hướng đến ai/cái gì để cầu cứu hoặc bám víu.
🙋‍♂️ / 🔊
Turn up
Xuất hiện, đến / Vặn to lên = arrive / appear unexpectedly
"She didn't turn up for class today."
"I expect a new job will turn up soon."
💡 Tip: Up = Trồi lên, xuất hiện bất ngờ.
