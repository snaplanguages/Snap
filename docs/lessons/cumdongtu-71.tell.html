Unit: TELL

Master Phrasal Verbs with TELL
💬
Tell about
Nói về ai đó hoặc cái gì = speak or relate information about sb or st
"The police wanted me to tell them about my friends."
💡 Tip: About = Xoay quanh chủ đề / sự việc
⚖️
Tell against
Làm chứng chống lại / Gây bất lợi = make sb or st more likely to fail
"His reputation as a troublemaker told against him when he tried to change his job."
💡 Tip: Against = Chống lại, gây trở ngại
🔍
Tell apart
Phân biệt sự khác nhau giữa 2 người/vật = see the difference between two very similar things or people
"As babies, the twins looked so much alike that I just couldn't tell them apart."
💡 Tip: Apart = Tách rời hai thứ giống nhau
🎨
Tell from
Phân biệt ai/cái gì với ai/cái gì = distinguish st as being distinct and unique compared to st
"Because of my color blindness, I can't tell shades of red from shades of green."
💡 Tip: From = Phân biệt A ra khỏi B
🤬
Tell off
La mắng, giận dữ nói với ai vì họ làm sai = speak angrily to sb because they have done st wrong
"The teacher told me off for swearing."
💡 Tip: Off = Quát tháo xối xả
🤫
Tell on
Phát giác, tố giác hành vi xấu của ai = give information about one's bad behavior or secrets
"If you don't stop hitting, I'm going to tell on you."
💡 Tip: On = Mách quéo, mách lẻo
